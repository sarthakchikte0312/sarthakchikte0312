01 — About

A hardware engineer's habit of taking things apart to understand them — then building better ones.

I'm a final-year Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering student at AISSMS IOIT, Pune, with hands-on experience across embedded systems, PCB design, RF engineering and firmware development. My work spans schematic capture and layout to the bare-metal C that runs on the board once it's populated.

Most recently I interned at Vadactro Technologies, debugging signal validation issues and training an image-detection pipeline for an IR-based smart-home remote system. Outside of that, I lead hardware design on Marut — India's first fully open-source tri-mode flight control unit — from PCB architecture through to sensor-driver firmware.

I'm part of Team AeroGuardians, and when I'm not routing traces I'm usually captaining the AISSMS IOIT volleyball team or writing Linux-flavoured captions for the internet.

In Development

Marut FCU

India's first fully open-source tri-mode Flight Control Unit

A UAV flight controller built around the STM32F411CEU6, designed to run three distinct flight modes from a single unified hardware and firmware framework — engineered as a low-cost, accessible platform for student teams.

  • Led schematic capture, PCB layout, and prototyping across both through-hole and SMD variants.
  • Currently developing firmware: sensor drivers, IMU/barometer integration, and mode-switching logic.
  • Optimized connector and power architecture for compact, manufacturable builds.
STM32F411CEU6KiCadCSensor Fusion

Jan – Mar 2026

Double-Redundant Variometer

Kalman & complementary filter sensor fusion

A real-time STM32F4XX-based variometer that fuses IMU and barometric data to estimate vertical velocity with low latency, displayed on a Nextion HMI.

  • Custom PCB integrating an MPU6050 IMU with dual BMP280 barometers for automatic failover.
  • Kalman + complementary filtering to reduce drift in dynamic flight conditions.
STM32F4XXMPU6050BMP280Nextion

Oct – Nov 2025

Vajra

16-sensor array line follower robot

An advanced PID-controlled line follower built around the ESP32-S3-Mini and a custom 16-sensor TCRT5000 array PCB.

  • Embedded C firmware handling ADC sampling, GPIO control, and real-time sensor fusion.
  • Custom 3D-modelled chassis in FreeCAD; debugged with an oscilloscope and logic analyzer.

STM32 Blackpill Driver Hub

Community bare-metal driver library

A community-driven hub of bare-metal drivers for STM32 Blackpill boards, written strictly to the ARM-CMSIS standard with zero HAL or CubeMX abstraction.

  • Contributed MQ-series gas sensor drivers modelled on their mathematical gas-chart curves.
  • Implemented in CMSIS-LL for direct register-level performance and control.

A self-designed KiCad development board for TI's MSPM0L1105, built end-to-end from schematic to a manufacturable, USB-C-powered board.
